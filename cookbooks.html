How these differ from the reference cookbooks
The CLI, SDK and contracts cookbooks show every operation once. These recipes start from a goal, take ten to twenty minutes end to end, and say what to do when a step fails on testnet.

Every recipe assumes a funded Sui testnet wallet. If you do not have one yet, start with the first recipe, it walks through the faucet.

Conventions

You seeIt means
$ commandType it in a shell. The Copy button strips the prompt.
0x69d4...f327A shortened id. Yours will differ, never copy these.
--via-aggregatorRead through the public Walrus aggregator. Reliable on testnet, trusts the operator.
--epochs 14How long Walrus keeps the bytes. One epoch is about a day on testnet.
Active contextThe publication and collection morse status shows. Set with morse use.
Testnet first
Morse runs on Walrus Mainnet and testnet. These recipes use testnet: it resets, blobs expire, tokens are free and worthless. Treat everything you publish as a rehearsal. Mainnet spends real SUI and WAL, so get a recipe working here first.
