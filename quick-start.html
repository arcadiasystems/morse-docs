Every path needs a funded testnet wallet

All three guides sign transactions on Sui testnet and store content on Walrus testnet. You need SUI for gas and WAL for storage. Both are free and take about two minutes.

StepWhereWhy
1. Install a walletSui Wallet, Suiet, or any Sui-compatible walletHolds your key and signs transactions
2. Get testnet SUIfaucet.sui.ioGas for every on-chain action
3. Swap some SUI for WALstake-wal.wal.appWalrus storage fees when you publish
Testnet first
Contracts are unaudited and testnets reset periodically. Testnet tokens have no monetary value. Morse also runs on mainnet, where gas and storage cost real SUI and WAL and there is no faucet. Run each guide on testnet before you try mainnet.
